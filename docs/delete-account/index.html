Delete your Bloom account

Bloom · last updated 8 October 2026

The quickest way is in the app: open Bloom, go to Profile → Your data → Delete account, and type DELETE to confirm. Your account and your household's data are removed from our server straight away.

If you can't open the app, email kushagra305@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in to Bloom with, with the subject "Delete my Bloom account". We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your account (name, email, profile picture link), your household and its members, invite codes, all locked diary records and photos, and scan counts. Copies in routine server backups expire on the hosting provider's normal backup schedule. If you are a partner, only your own account and membership are deleted; the household stays with its owner.

What stays: the copy of Bloom's data on a phone stays until the app is deleted from that phone. Deleting the account in the app clears it on that phone.
